# Independent core architecture audit — 2026-10-07

Status: research handoff; **no production code changed, no tests or benchmarks executed during this review**.
Audited repository: [main at 0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/commit/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2). The earlier October 7 audit was committed in this same head. Existing ADRs remain accepted and binding until individually superseded.
Audience: implementation engineers, architecture reviewers, competition operators.

## Executive result

Bunting already has a real Rust engine foundation: integer-domain types; one venue-authority crate; listing-keyed matcher snapshots; participant ownership; checked zero-fee trade settlement; explicit risk; canonical event envelopes; simulation lifecycle commands; deterministic agent policies; FIX session support; a native acceptor; a bounded writer gate; an in-memory and whole-file origin; CLI replay of a limited archive format; and RIT/QUARCC references. **Those pieces do not yet form a correct multi-venue, multi-day economic simulation.**

Priority must be determined by dependency, not component popularity: (1) an explicit economic-state and listing-identity contract, including one authoritative fill-posting operation; (2) live state and transaction/recovery design; (3) complete execution/fan-out and multi-session lifecycle; (4) representative calibrated market behavior; (5) product surfaces. Address real functional gaps directly and write focused invariant tests with each implementation, rather than building a separate general regression infrastructure first.

Evidence vocabulary: **Observed** = present in the audited source or recorded checked-in test; **Reasoned** = design inference, not benchmark proof; **External** = linked independent implementation/paper/documentation; **Unresolved** = requires a spike, representative workload, or failure injection. Source inspection here does not imply compilation or test execution.

### Reproduced source-level failures and their consequences

**A. Cross-listing rejection, not just cosmetic routing.** SubmitOrder.instrument_id + listing_key_for_instrument mean a repeated InstrumentId on two venues triggers AmbiguousListing. Cancel uses stored ownership to recover venue. Public depth, trade tape, risk price bounds and halt controls additionally use InstrumentId in important places. Source: [commands](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/market-events/src/lib.rs#L103-L119), [lookup](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/bunting-engine/src/lib.rs#L719-L733), [risk restore](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/bunting-engine/src/lib.rs#L1076-L1090), [depth](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/bunting-engine/src/simulation.rs#L1020-L1034). This blocks coherent cross-listing even though BTreeMap<ListingKey, ListingState> exists.

**B. Inconsistent ledger of record.** Normal matching uses Ledger::settle_trade; SimulationState::project_event updates market trades and private order state, not PortfolioLedger trade books. Fines/cashflow/facilities post or adjust PortfolioLedger independently; score_iteration reads it. A fill, position, fee, marking and score therefore lack a single transactional source. Source: [fills](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/bunting-engine/src/lib.rs#L1630-L1704), [trade projection](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/bunting-engine/src/simulation.rs#L1000-L1016), [fine](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/bunting-engine/src/simulation.rs#L866-L907), [score](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/bunting-engine/src/simulation.rs#L1193-L1232). A correct basic trade is not evidence of a correct final competition score.

**C. High copying and lookup complexity.** RunState::transition clones RunState and both ledger projections; restore_book deserializes an OrderBook-rs snapshot; replace_snapshot serializes it again; refresh_market_projection walks ownership twice and sorts active orders. apply_trades discovers maker_id by a linear values().find for each fill. FileOriginStore additionally clones the complete FileState and rewrites JSON, including prior events, for every command. Source: [transition](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/bunting-engine/src/lib.rs#L784-L812), [projection](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/bunting-engine/src/lib.rs#L1092-L1150), [restoration](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/bunting-engine/src/lib.rs#L1152-L1202), [maker search](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/bunting-engine/src/lib.rs#L1638-L1649), [disk](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/apps/bunting-server/src/storage.rs#L166-L214). This is structurally superlinear as live orders/history grow, though its absolute overhead is **not measured** here.

**D. Replay and durability boundaries weaker than claim.** The file write synchronizes the temporary file, then renames without visibly synchronizing the parent directory; power-failure durable acknowledgement must be empirically established. CompetitionArchive declares accepted_commands: Vec<SimulationCommandRequest>, so ordinary matching cannot be rebuilt by that verifier. A canonical archive must include every mutating admitted command, admission sequence and deterministic exogenous event. Source: [storage](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/apps/bunting-server/src/storage.rs#L203-L214), [archive](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/bunting-rs/src/archive.rs#L17-L28), [replay](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/bunting-rs/src/archive.rs#L91-L130).

**E. Fairness is not the ADR-described algorithm.** ADR 0024 describes release of a shared interval batch. The current AuthoritativeWriter delays each queued caller until a boundary but then executes successive actions in arrival order, potentially extending through later intervals. Order priority remains arrival-first, and interval membership/scheduler perturbations are not durably recorded. Fairness must define its intended property (e.g. deterministic FCFS vs randomized uniform batch rank vs batch call auction), not advertise equality of network latency from sleeping. Source: [ADR 0024](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/docs/adr/0024-discrete-matching-interval-fairness.md), [writer](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/apps/bunting-server/src/writer.rs#L26-L79).

**F. FIX participant ownership not delivered symmetrically.** Session loop maps newly committed events into committed_messages for the submitting credential and writes those messages back to the same TcpStream. A passive maker on another connection is not part of this send loop; no evidenced shared dispatcher publishes that maker's unsolicited fills. Connection-local open_orders starts empty and only tracks accepted submits/cancels on that socket; it is not the authoritative number of active orders. Source: [session](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/apps/bunting-server/src/session_host.rs#L128-L297). Reconnecting a participant does not necessarily restore private economic order state into the risk/admission counter.

**G. Scheduler process recovery is incomplete.** The deterministic runtime supports snapshots/restoration as a library, but server scenario::run constructs DeterministicRuntime::new on startup and drives it with thread sleep; engine-side origin snapshots do not visibly checkpoint the scheduler together with the state transition. This threatens repeatability of agent wakes after a server crash. Source: [runtime snapshot](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/bunting-runtime/src/lib.rs), [server scenario](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/apps/bunting-server/src/scenario.rs#L60-L80).

**H. RIT-like workflows contain status-only implementations.** CompositeCompleted records a count without matching/settling legs. Tender accept and OTC accept change state and emit events; a financially meaningful transfer requires explicit posting, availability checks, and settlement. InstrumentKind exists but options exercise/delivery is not demonstrated. Source: [simulation handlers](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/bunting-engine/src/simulation.rs#L727-L835), [composite](https://github.com/andrewkoumoudjian/bunting/blob/0fdbd130a59b212ac6cae1d3b78000acd94fb9b2/packages/bunting-engine/src/simulation.rs#L1161-L1191). Label these states or scaffolding, not complete markets.

### Evidence supporting the alternatives

- [OrderBook-rs 0.10.3 API/release](https://docs.rs/crate/orderbook-rs/0.10.3), [upstream repository](https://github.com/joaquinbejar/OrderBook-rs): order kinds, snapshots and native concurrency. Upstream performance statements are **not Bunting benchmarks**. Its concurrent design is useful if matching must support concurrent mutation, but Bunting's single-run commit order may not benefit from internal concurrent structures.
- [matchcore](https://docs.rs/matchcore/latest/matchcore/): independently documented single-threaded deterministic Rust matching alternative. Validate version maturity, supported order semantics and license before considering replacement.
- [LMAX architecture](https://martinfowler.com/articles/lmax.html), [LMAX Disruptor](https://github.com/LMAX-Exchange/disruptor): single-owner memory design and bounded queues. Performance claims are specific to LMAX; do not transpose them to Bunting.
- [SQLite WAL](https://www.sqlite.org/wal.html) and [synchronous durability](https://www.sqlite.org/pragma.html#pragma_synchronous): SQLite WAL with FULL synchronizes each committing WAL transaction; NORMAL can lose latest commits on an OS/power crash. Disk-level guarantees depend on real filesystem and storage stack. This is an engineering alternative, not a settled dependency.
- [ABIDES](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.12066) / [original GitHub](https://github.com/abides-sim/abides): discrete-event multi-agent architecture with configurable network latency. [JPMC public fork](https://github.com/jpmorganchase/abides-jpmc-public) is archived (2025); use as research oracle, not wholesale Rust port.
- [Cont, Stoikov & Talreja (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.1090.0780) and [Huang, Lehalle & Rosenbaum (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.2014.982278): estimable queue/reactive order-book dynamics for staged realism; neither implies that calibrated agents reproduce all empirical market behavior.
- [Chen & Duffie (2021), Market Fragmentation](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20200829) and [crossed/locked multi-market simulation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4784946/): cross-venue depth and routing change prices and liquidity; consolidated tape must not replace independent books.
- [Nasdaq Rule 4757](https://listingcenter.nasdaq.com/rulebook/nasdaq/rules/Nasdaq%20Equity%204) and [reserve-order priority](https://listingcenter.nasdaq.com/rulebook/ise/rules/ise-options-3): real market priorities depend on displayed/hidden behavior and venue rules, not a universal 'FIFO' promise.
- [Wasmer WASIX docs](https://docs.wasmer.io/runtime/wasix/): WASIX provides sockets/threads beyond plain WASI. Compatibility alone does not prove engine performance or durability.

### What to retain, adapt, or stop

**Retain now:** checked integer primitives and ListingKey; central engine authority; direct privately encapsulated OrderBook-rs adapter as baseline; clean participant/venue separation; pure command-to-event semantics; scenario/version hashes; risk policy contract; Rust FIX codecs/session parser; source/provenance and clean-room RIT/NBC audit; existing conformance fixtures; TUI as diagnostic client; Cloudflare only as publication adapter.

**Modify:** SubmitOrder and all listing-sensitive commands/events/projections; portfolio journal and reservations; RunState owning live book(s) with transaction boundary; origin persistence and indexes; archive input/completeness; native writer admission sequencing; maker/taker/private report delivery; scheduler checkpoint; calendar; versioned mark/score; slow-consumer and reconnect protocol.

**Eliminate or defer:** per-command all-run clone/book restore/full-store JSON rewrite on hot path; separate un-reconciled accounting authority; connection-local open-order authority; claimed interval batching without interval records; RIT parity labels for state-only workflows; advanced UI expansion until engine acceptance.
